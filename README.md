Module Breakdown & Layout Rules
Section 0: System Initialization (Pages 001–024)
 * Visual Styling: High-contrast linework on heavy black/charcoal paper stock transitions.
 * Base Layout: Incorporates the right-side Ellipsis (...) structural key, framework taxonomy declaration (Human = Standing/Living Constraint Model; System = Adaptive Constraint Model), and acronym ledger (Materials Affect Design, Made of Momentum, Methods and Deployment).
Section I: Narrative & Living Constraint Foundation (Pages 025–144)
 * Visual Styling: Classic medieval ink calligraphy, gold leaf accents, and ornate filigree framing.
 * Base Layout: Narrative text running through the body of each page, grounded by dense historical and conceptual context at the page bottoms.
Section II: Technical Specifications & Invention Schematics (Pages 145–284)
 * Visual Styling: Precision CAD-style schematic drawings paired with crisp gothic text headers.
 * Base Layout: Includes technical breakdowns for modular palm/wrist/forearm bands (M.A.D. Grips+), site-adaptive frameworks, and thermal/ergonomic reinforcement structures.
Section III: Tension-Relief & Self-Audit Annotations (Pages 285–364)
 * Visual Styling: Dense body text paired with multi-directional handwritten ink marginalia along top, side, and bottom margins.
 * Base Layout: Intersperses high-CPU thread logs with self-effacing humor ("mental patient" jokes, traffic detour survival logs, and cognitive defragmentation notes) to break tension directly on the page.
Section IV: Interactive Exploration & Micro-Fiber Canvas (Pages 365–428)
 * Visual Styling: Minimalist structural lines integrated with natural watermark micro-fibers embedded in the paper stock.
 * Base Layout: Cartoon panels and open canvas areas designed for kids and system navigators to trace micro-fibers, draw inventions, and add their own marginalia.
